# Water Systems Pack release 4

- Pack ID is `studio.techpanda.water_tanks`, schema version is 1, and pack release is 4. The three previous items (`rectangular_water_tank`, `cylindrical_water_tank`, and `ro_plant`) retain their IDs and definitions.
- Added `underground_concrete_water_tank`, default 3 x 2 x 2 m, with concrete base/walls/slab, inspection access, pipe connections, and level/depth/volume plus optional pump/low/overflow slots. No pump control is included.
- Place the tank on Ground Floor. The origin is at the bottom; `mount_y = -2 m` places a 2 m tank from -2 m through the floor surface. Use **Set tank top at floor level** for the common burial setup. Resizing preserves the configured top elevation.
- The default-off persisted **Underground tanks** Show-menu toggle temporarily cuts a rotated opening in the floor above each negative-height tank and shows tank geometry only during inspection. Disabling the mode restores the original floor mesh. It does not add a floor or room or modify the saved plan geometry.
- Water measurement supports percentage, depth with unit/depth calibration, and measured volume with unit/capacity calibration. Invalid or missing values hide live fill. The editor reports internal usable dimensions, assumed wall and slab thicknesses, burial depth, geometric/calibrated capacity, measured/estimated volume, remaining capacity and read-only status entities.
- Run the production viewer check with `node techpanda/furniture/underground-tank-viewer-preview.mjs`. It writes actual production screenshots and a verification record to ignored `techpanda/furniture/dist/underground-tank-viewer-previews/`; visually inspect them for multiple tanks, water levels, the floor views, and settings.
- Signed local artifact: `techpanda/furniture/dist/techpanda-water-tanks-v4.fp3dpack`; SHA-256 is recorded in this folder's `water-systems-v4.sha256`. Build and verify with the existing key using `Build-NeonFurniture`, `Test-NeonFurniturePack`, and `Test-NeonFurniturePackSecurity`. Never regenerate or expose the private key.
- The furniture asset and downstream v4.0.2.4b7 integration remain prepared locally until separately published. No HA plan/configuration, remote integration, pump, or Home Assistant service was modified or operated.
